About Unity Fund

We believe more people would give well if giving well were simple. Unity Fund makes a diversified, carefully researched portfolio of charities available to anyone, through a single contribution.

Mission

Our mission is to move more money to effective organizations by making diversified giving simple, transparent and free of fees.

Why a fund

Most people who give want their money to matter, but few have the time to compare hundreds of organizations, read audited financial statements and follow up on results. The organizations doing the most good are often not the ones with the largest marketing budgets.

Investors faced a similar problem decades ago, and the index fund solved it: one purchase, broad diversification, low cost and full disclosure. Unity Fund applies the same idea to charitable giving. Contributors give once, and a published allocation does the rest.

The fund opened in January 2023 with 31 organizations. Today it holds 39 and has distributed $16.8 million on behalf of more than 14,200 contributors.

What we hold ourselves to

  • Contributions are for the mission

    No fee is taken from any contribution. Our operating costs are raised separately, from supporters who give specifically to keep the fund running.

  • Everything is published

    Holdings, weights, grants, removals, operating costs and committee decisions are all public. If we would not want to publish a decision, we should not make it.

  • Selection is independent

    Organizations cannot pay or apply to be included, and the people who choose them have no financial ties to any holding.

  • Breadth over bets

    The fund is built to be a sound foundation for anyone's giving, spread across many needs and many organizations rather than concentrated in a few.

Governance

Unity Fund is governed by an independent board of directors, which approves the methodology, appoints the allocation committee and oversees the annual audit. No director is compensated, and no director may hold a role with any organization in the portfolio.

Policies

The commitments we make to every contributor. Full policy documents are available on request.

  • Donor privacy

    We never sell, rent or trade contributor information. Your name appears in published contributor lists only if you choose, and you may ask us to delete your records at any time, subject to the records we are required to keep for tax purposes.

  • Gift acceptance

    We accept cash, securities, grants from donor-advised funds and qualified charitable distributions from IRAs. Every accepted gift enters the fund pool. Gifts with conditions on how they must be used are declined.

  • Conflicts of interest

    Directors, committee members and staff disclose potential conflicts every year and step aside from any decision where one exists. Disclosures are summarized in each annual report.

  • Whistleblower protection

    Anyone may report concerns about the fund or a holding to the board chair in confidence, without fear of retaliation.
