How the fund works

The same four steps, repeated every quarter since the fund opened in 2023.

  1. You contribute

    Give once or on a schedule by card, bank transfer, stock, donor-advised fund or IRA distribution. You receive one tax receipt for the full amount.

  2. Gifts are pooled

    Every contribution received during the quarter joins a single pool. Nothing is deducted for our own costs, which are funded separately.

  3. The pool is distributed

    On the 15th of the month after each quarter closes, the full pool is granted to every organization in the portfolio at its published weight.

  4. Every grant is reported

    Each distribution is published line by line, and every contributor receives a statement showing where their share went.

How organizations are chosen

No organization can pay or apply to be included. Candidates are nominated by researchers, program evaluators and existing holdings, then reviewed against four standards by an allocation committee with no financial ties to any of them.

Read the full methodology

  1. Evidence of results

    Programs are supported by independent evaluation or rigorous monitoring data, not anecdotes alone.

  2. Cost effectiveness

    Each dollar achieves more than it would at comparable organizations doing similar work.

  3. Transparency

    Audited financial statements, public reporting and a willingness to share what did not work.

  4. Room for more funding

    Additional money will expand the work, rather than sit in reserves.

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Write to hello@unityfund.org and a member of our staff will reply within two business days.

Is my contribution tax deductible?

Yes. Unity Fund is a 501(c)(3) public charity, and contributions are deductible to the extent allowed by law. You receive a single receipt for the full amount, however many organizations it reaches.

How does Unity Fund pay for its own operations?

Our staff, research and systems are paid from a separate operating account, supported by founding donors and foundation grants. Contributions to the fund itself are never used for operating costs.

Can I choose which cause areas receive my money?

No. Every contribution follows the same published allocation, which keeps the fund diversified, simple and inexpensive to run. If you want to support one organization in particular, every holding is listed on the portfolio page so you can give to it directly.

When does my money reach the organizations?

Contributions are pooled for the quarter and distributed on the 15th of January, April, July and October. A gift made today is included in the next distribution.

What happens when an organization is removed?

Its share is divided among the remaining holdings in the same cause area until the next rebalance. Every removal, and the reason for it, is published on the portfolio page.

Can I give stock, or give from a donor-advised fund or IRA?

Yes. We accept grants from donor-advised funds, qualified charitable distributions from IRAs, transfers of appreciated stock and bank transfers. Instructions are on the contribution page.
