    Other ways to give

    Every method below goes into the same pool and is divided the same way. For help with any of them, write to gifts@unityfund.org.

    • Donor-advised fund

      Recommend a grant to Unity Fund from your donor-advised fund. Use our legal name, Unity Fund, and our EIN, 00-0000000.

    • Stock and securities

      Giving appreciated stock held for more than a year can avoid capital gains tax. Email us for brokerage transfer instructions.

    • IRA distribution

      If you are 70½ or older, a qualified charitable distribution from your IRA can count toward your required minimum distribution.

    • Bank transfer

      For contributions of $10,000 or more, we provide wire and ACH instructions so that no card processing cost applies.

    • Check

      Make checks payable to Unity Fund, and include your email address so we can send your receipt and allocation statement.

    • Employer matching

      Many employers match charitable gifts. Search for Unity Fund in your company's giving portal, or ask us for a matching form.

    Tax information

    Unity Fund is a tax-exempt public charity under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, EIN 00-0000000. Contributions are tax deductible to the extent allowed by law. No goods or services are provided in exchange for a contribution.

    You receive one receipt for each contribution, however many organizations it reaches. Recurring contributors also receive a single annual summary each January.
